Our story

A shared mission to make our world greener.

The Team Green project brings people together around tree planting, forest conservation, and practical ways to support a more sustainable future.

📍 Brahmadevam, Nellore, Andhra Pradesh

Our mission

Make tree planting easier for everyone.

The project encourages citizens and organizations to take part in tree planting, reforestation, and conservation. Trees store carbon as they grow, but a healthy, lasting impact depends on choosing suitable trees and caring for them over time.

Through awareness and practical community projects, we aim to make planting and maintaining trees more accessible while supporting local communities and protecting biodiversity.

Weekly activities

Eight weeks from planning to plant care

Follow the project from its first community survey through planting, monitoring, and sharing the results.

Week 1

Plan the project

Form the team, set project goals, and identify suitable places for planting in the community.

Week 2

Survey the locality

Talk with residents, note areas that need more greenery, and seek permission for the planned activities.

Week 3

Raise awareness

Share information about the value of trees with students and residents using posters, pamphlets, and a banner.

Week 4

Prepare to plant

Choose suitable saplings, arrange tools, and prepare planting locations with help from the community.

Week 5

Plant the saplings

Plant at selected community and school locations, including the road area and DLNR High School in Buchireddypalem.

Week 6

Distribute & protect

Share plants with residents, water young trees, add compost, and protect saplings from animal damage.

Week 7

Monitor plant health

Check growth and soil moisture, identify problems, and coordinate ongoing care with local helpers.

Week 8

Document the results

Record the project outcomes, collect photographs, reflect on lessons, and share the work through this website.
