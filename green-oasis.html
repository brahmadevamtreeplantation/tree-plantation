Our story

A community effort that continues after planting.

This student-led Community Service Project combined a local survey, tree-planting awareness, sapling distribution, and care for newly planted trees.

The project planted 10 saplings near DLNR High School in Buchireddypalem and encouraged residents to take part in caring for plants in their community.

Plants & offset

Why caring for trees matters

Trees can benefit local places and wildlife. Choosing suitable plants and helping them survive are important parts of a planting effort.

🌳

Greener shared spaces

Trees add greenery and shade to the places where people live and gather.

🦋

Support local nature

Suitable trees can provide food and habitat for birds, insects, and other wildlife.

💧

Keep caring as they grow

Watering and protecting young plants help give saplings a better chance to establish.

Project approach

Awareness, planting, and follow-up

The project joined local outreach with hands-on planting and monitoring in the community.

What the team did

  • Surveyed the community and identified places for more greenery.
  • Held an awareness camp and shared posters and pamphlets.
  • Planted 10 saplings near DLNR High School.
  • Distributed plants and encouraged residents to care for them.

What the team learned

  • Young trees need consistent watering, especially in hot weather.
  • Protection from animal damage can help safeguard new growth.
  • Compost was used as a natural fertilizer for the plants.
  • Follow-up care matters after a planting activity.

Week-by-week activities

Eight weeks from planning to plant care

Follow the project from its first community survey through planting, monitoring, and sharing the results.

Week 1

Plan the project

Form the team, set project goals, and identify suitable places for community planting.

Week 2

Survey the locality

Talk with residents, note areas that need greenery, and seek permission for planned activities.

Week 3

Raise awareness

Share information about trees with students and residents using an awareness camp, posters, and pamphlets.

Week 4

Prepare to plant

Choose suitable saplings, arrange tools, and prepare planting locations with the community.

Week 5

Plant the saplings

Plant at selected locations, including near the road and DLNR High School in Buchireddypalem.

Week 6

Distribute and protect

Share plants with residents, water young trees, add compost, and protect saplings from animal damage.

Week 7

Monitor plant health

Check growth and soil moisture, identify problems, and coordinate ongoing care with local helpers.

Week 8

Document the results

Record outcomes, collect photographs, reflect on lessons, and share the work through the website.

Monitoring report: Of the 10 saplings planted, 6 were reported in good condition during a later check and 4 had died.
